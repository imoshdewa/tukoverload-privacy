TukOverload privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

TukOverload is a game for young children, published on Google Play as com.imoshdewa.tukoverload. We built it to collect nothing.

What we collect: nothing

The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and it does not send anything about you or your device to us or to anyone else. We do not share any data with anyone, because we have none.

What stays on the phone

The game remembers which levels you have opened, your stars and best scores, whether the sound is on, whether Christmas is switched on, and whether the full game has been bought. This is saved only on the phone, in the game's own storage, and is deleted when the game is uninstalled.

Purchases

One part of the game (the full game) can be bought. Buying happens on Google Play's own payment screen: Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy, and the game never sees any payment details. The game only asks Google Play whether the full game has been bought on this Google account, so it can open it (for example on a new phone). A grown-up has to answer a question before the payment screen opens.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
